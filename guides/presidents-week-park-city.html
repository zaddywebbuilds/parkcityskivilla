Presidents’ Week in Park City, 2027

The busiest ski week of the Utah season, why it books out so far ahead, and what it actually costs.

Guide · Updated October 2026

Presidents’ Day 2027 falls on Monday 15 February. The week around it is the hardest stretch of the Utah season to book, and the reason is not really the holiday itself. It is that school February breaks across the north-east and midwest land in the same window, so families who ski once a year all travel at once.

One thing has changed for 2027 that most guides have not caught up with. Park City’s visitor guides have long described Presidents’ week as the busiest week of the year after Sundance. The Sundance Film Festival left Park City for Boulder, Colorado from 2027, with January 2026 its final Utah edition. With the festival gone, Presidents’ week stands as the single busiest week on the Park City calendar.

Why this week is different

Three things stack up at the same time. Presidents’ Day gives a three-day federal weekend. Most north-eastern school districts run a February recess in the same week. And mid-February is reliably deep in the Utah snowpack, well past the thin early-season cover and before the spring thaw starts affecting lower runs.

The result is that Park City Mountain and Deer Valley both run near capacity, lift lines are at their longest of the year, and lodging prices reach their seasonal peak. A two-bedroom villa that sits quiet in early December or late March is booked out months ahead for this one week, and ski-in units at Canyons Village are the first to go.

The crowds do not stop on the Monday

This catches people out. A common plan is to book the three-day weekend and leave on the Monday evening, assuming the town empties afterwards. It does not. Park City’s own chamber has described bookings running past the holiday weekend and well into the following week, because the school breaks driving the demand are full weeks, not long weekends. If you are travelling to avoid crowds, shifting by two or three days will not do it; you need a different week.

The units that disappear first are the ones that sleep six to eight, because they suit the two-family trips this week is built around. Studios and one-bedrooms stay available much longer.

What to look for in a Presidents’ Week rental

Walking distance to a lift, genuinely

“Ski-in/ski-out” is used loosely. In a week this busy, parking at the base is slow and shuttles are full, so the difference between a true slopeside property and one that is a ten-minute drive is an hour a day, twice.

A kitchen you would actually cook in

Restaurant tables in Park City during Presidents’ week need booking well ahead, and feeding eight people out for seven nights is a significant cost on top of the lodging. A full kitchen is the difference between eating out by choice and by necessity.

In-unit laundry

Seven days of ski gear for a group generates more washing than people expect. Properties without laundry mean either packing twice as much or paying resort rates.

Separate living space

Two families sharing one open-plan room for a week is where trips go wrong. Units with two distinct living areas, rather than two bedrooms off a single lounge, hold up far better across seven nights.

What else is on that month

Two things worth knowing if you are planning around events rather than just the skiing.

The Freestyle International FIS World Cup returns to Deer Valley on 28–30 January 2027, with moguls, dual moguls and aerials on the courses built for the 2002 Olympics. Two FIS NorAm Cups follow in early February, at Utah Olympic Park on the 3rd and 4th and Deer Valley on the 5th and 6th. All of these finish before Presidents’ week starts, so they are a reason to come earlier rather than something you will catch during the holiday.

The Sundance Film Festival is no longer in Park City. After 44 years the festival moved to Boulder, Colorado, with January 2026 being the final Utah edition. Any guide still telling you Park City hosts Sundance in January is out of date. It does change the character of the town that month, and it is worth knowing before you book a January trip on that basis.

Getting there

Salt Lake City International is roughly 45 minutes by car and has direct flights from most major US cities, which is a large part of why Park City draws the volume it does for a single holiday week. There is more detail in our guide to getting from SLC airport to Park City, including the shuttle and rental trade-off in winter conditions.

The short version

  • Book larger units 6–12 months ahead, they go first
  • Expect $800–$1,200 a night for a two-bedroom near the lifts
  • Prioritise true lift access, a real kitchen, laundry, and separate living areas
  • The Deer Valley World Cup is late January, before the holiday week
  • Sundance has left Park City from 2027 onward
