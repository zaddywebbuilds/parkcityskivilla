Salt Lake City Airport to Park City: Every Way to Make the Transfer

Published 27 September 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

One of the genuine advantages of skiing Utah is that the airport is close. Canyons Village is about 33 miles from the terminal, which is a different category of journey from most ski trips.

Salt Lake City International sits roughly 33 miles from Canyons Village, about 40 to 45 minutes in normal conditions. The route is I-80 east through Parley's Canyon, then Highway 224 towards Park City.

For comparison, plenty of European and North American ski resorts involve two to four hours from the nearest useful airport. Landing and being on snow the same afternoon is realistic here, and that changes how you plan the first and last days.

What changes the drive time

Two things, and both of them matter during February.

  • Snow in Parley's Canyon. The canyon is where weather slows things down. A storm adds 15 to 30 minutes routinely, and occasionally much more if the road is restricted.
  • Traffic. Weekday evenings out of Salt Lake, and Friday afternoons in ski season, both add time. Saturday morning, when the whole resort changes over, is its own event.
For the flight home

Leave earlier than feels necessary. A delay in the canyon is the single most common way people miss a flight out of Salt Lake, and it happens during exactly the weeks when the flights are full and rebooking is hard.

Do the per-person maths

A private transfer looks expensive next to a shared shuttle until you divide it. For a group of eight travelling together with ski bags, the private van is often similar in total and considerably better in practice, because it leaves when you land rather than when it fills.

This is the same arithmetic that makes one large unit beat two hotel rooms, and it applies to most of the fixed costs on a group ski trip.

Driving yourself

The route is genuinely easy. There are no high passes between the airport and Park City, which surprises people expecting alpine switchbacks. In normal conditions it is motorway and a main road.

In a storm it is still a mountain canyon. Check conditions before you set off, make sure the rental has proper winter tyres, and understand that traction restrictions can be imposed at short notice. If you are not comfortable driving in snow, this is an argument for letting somebody else do it.

If you are staying in the village and skiing the mountain, you may not need a car at all.

Common questions

How far is Park City from Salt Lake City airport?

Canyons Village is roughly 33 miles from Salt Lake City International Airport, about 40 to 45 minutes in normal conditions via I-80 through Parley's Canyon and then Highway 224.

How much time should I allow for the drive in winter?

Add 15 to 30 minutes for snow in Parley's Canyon, and more if a storm is active or traffic is heavy. For a departing flight during a holiday week, leave well ahead of what the map suggests.

Is there a shuttle from Salt Lake City airport to Canyons Village?

Yes. Shared shuttles and private transfers both serve Park City and Canyons Village, and several operators run them. Shared is cheaper per person, private leaves on your schedule and often costs less in total for a large group.
