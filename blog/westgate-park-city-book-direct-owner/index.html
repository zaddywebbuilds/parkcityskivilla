Booking a Westgate Park City Villa Direct from the Owner

Published 30 September 2026 Park City, Utah 6 min read

The same villa can appear on three booking sites at three different prices. Understanding why explains most of what you need to know about booking direct.

On this page
  1. What booking direct changes
  2. What to ask before you send money
  3. Timeshare and whole ownership
  4. Reading a listing honestly

Most individual units at Westgate Park City, like most of what you find on the large rental platforms, are owned by individuals and rented out by them rather than by the resort itself.

That structure is why the same unit turns up in several places at several prices. The owner sets a rate, and each platform adds its own service fee on top for the guest, plus a commission from the owner.

What booking direct changes

  • The platform fee goes away. On a seven night peak-week booking that is a real number, not a rounding error.
  • You are talking to someone who has slept there. An owner can tell you which building it is, which lift you walk to and whether the second bedroom is genuinely a bedroom.
  • Dates can be discussed. Platforms enforce rigid calendars. An owner can sometimes shift a changeover day.

The trade is that you give up the platform's dispute process, so you do more of your own diligence. That is a fair exchange when the diligence is straightforward, which it is here.

What to ask before you send money

  1. Which building and unit, and how long is the walk to the gondola? Specific answers, not reassurances. Covered in more detail in the ski-in ski-out guide.
  2. What is the all-in total for my exact dates? Including cleaning, resort fees, any village assessment and tax. One number.
  3. What are the beds, precisely? Two kings and two sleeper sofas is a different thing from a king, a bunk and a futon, and both get described as sleeping eight.
  4. What is the cancellation policy, in writing? Peak-week bookings are made months ahead and plans change.
  5. How do I pay, and what is the receipt? A clear written agreement and a traceable payment method.

Timeshare and whole ownership

Westgate includes both timeshare intervals and whole-ownership condominium units, and owners of either may rent out their weeks. This does not usually affect your stay, but it is a reasonable thing to ask about, because it tells you how the owner comes to have those dates.

Reading a listing honestly

Vagueness is the signal. A listing that will not name the building, will not name the lift, and describes the beds loosely is telling you something. A listing that answers all three in a sentence each is usually run by somebody who actually uses the place.

Compare on one number

Ask every option for the all-in total for your dates, with every fee and tax included. A lower nightly rate frequently is not lower, and this is the only comparison that survives contact with checkout.

Common questions

Are Westgate Park City units rented by the resort or by owners?

Individual units are generally owned by individuals and rented out by them rather than by the resort. That is why the same unit can appear on several booking platforms at several different prices.

Is it cheaper to book a Park City villa direct?

Usually, because the booking platform's service fee is removed. On a seven night peak-week stay that difference is substantial.

What should I ask an owner before booking?

Which building the unit is in and how long the walk to the lift takes, the all-in total for your dates including all fees and tax, exactly what the beds are, the cancellation policy in writing, and how payment works.
