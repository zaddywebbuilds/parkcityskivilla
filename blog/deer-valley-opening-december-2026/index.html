Deer Valley Opens on 5 December, Ten Days After Park City

Published 23 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Deer Valley opening day 2026 is Saturday 5 December, conditions permitting. That is ten days after Park City Mountain, and the gap is the second thing worth knowing about the two resorts. The first is that one of them will not let you snowboard.

Is the 5 December opening date holding?

Yes, as far as anything in early December is settled. Deer Valley’s own winter page gives Saturday 5 December 2026, and a status update from resort leadership on 20 November had the resort still scheduled for that day, conditions permitting. The vice president of mountain operations said the team had made more snow in a single night after a storm that week than at any point since snowmaking began in November.

The hedging is real, though. Deer Valley has missed a target before: last season it aimed at late November and ended up opening in December after thin conditions. The resort has also said it could open earlier if the weather allows, so the date can move in both directions. Park City Mountain’s own opening is the cautionary tale this year, having slipped from 20 to 25 November.

What is new this season: Hail Peak and the east side

The Hail Peak lift went in over the summer, and it opens seven new runs on the east side of the resort this season, reached from the Deer Valley East Village drop-off. Resort staff describe the arrangement as direct to lift: a pass holder arriving at the East Village drop-off can get on Hail Peak Express without a shuttle leg in between.

What is not finished is the base around it. The East Village Lodge and the Park Peak lodge are both reported as targeting the following winter, with December 2027 named for Park Peak. So the new terrain is skiable this season but the buildings serving it are a year out, and anyone skiing that side in 2027 skis an active construction site at the bottom. No competitor lodging guide puts those two facts next to each other.

The rule that decides it for most families: skiers only

Deer Valley does not permit snowboarding. Going into this season its own rules still list snowboards among the devices that are not approved, and the restriction covers the entire resort, the new expansion terrain included.

If anybody in your group rides, that settles the question before any comparison of grooming, queues or restaurants. It is the single most consequential difference between the two resorts and the reason we treat it as the first question in choosing between Canyons Village and Deer Valley for a family week.

Deer Valley is on Ikon, not Epic

Your Park City Mountain pass does not work here. Deer Valley sits on the Ikon Pass, where a full Ikon gives up to seven days and each visit has to be reserved ahead through the pass website. Sources disagree on whether the cheaper Ikon Base Pass still includes Deer Valley for 2026-27, so check before buying on that basis.

Deer Valley also caps how many daily lift tickets it sells and recommends buying online in advance, which is a real operational difference from Park City Mountain and worth knowing if you plan to decide on the morning. The Epic side of the ledger, including this season’s 6 December sales deadline, is in what a week of lift access costs. We deliberately do not quote pass prices here, because the figures we found varied by several hundred dollars between sources.

Skiing Deer Valley from Canyons Village

This is the practical case for staying on our side of town and driving over for a day or two. Canyons Village puts you walking distance from the lifts at the larger resort for most of the week, and Deer Valley becomes a day trip rather than the thing you pay a premium to sit next to.

On the drive, we will be honest about what we know: we have found no published door-to-door time from Canyons Village to Deer Valley. Our own estimate is twenty to thirty minutes depending on traffic and which Deer Valley base you aim for, and that is an estimate rather than a figure anyone has verified. Whether you need a car at all is worked through in do you need a car in Park City, and the wider comparison in Deer Valley versus Park City Mountain.

If you are planning a day over there

Reserve the Ikon day or buy the capped ticket before you travel, check the snowboard rule against your group, and pick a Deer Valley base before you set off rather than at the junction. The villa layout, if you are weighing where to stay, is in the floor plan.

Who should care about the 5 December date

Three groups. Anyone travelling in the first week of December who assumed the whole valley opens at once: it does not, and for ten days Park City Mountain is the only game in town. Anyone whose trip is built around Deer Valley specifically, who should treat 5 December as a plan rather than a promise and keep a cancellable booking. And anyone coming for the freestyle World Cup at Deer Valley in January, for whom the opening date is the start of a runway rather than the event.

Everybody else can file it as useful context. If your week is February or March, both resorts will be fully operating and the choice goes back to terrain, passes and whether anyone in the party rides.

Common questions

When does Deer Valley open for the 2026-27 season?

Saturday 5 December 2026, conditions permitting. The date is on the resort's own winter page, and a status update from resort leadership on 20 November still had it scheduled for that day. The resort has said it could open earlier if conditions allow, and it has delayed an opening in a previous season.

Why does Deer Valley open later than Park City Mountain?

They are separate businesses with their own snowmaking programmes and opening plans. Park City Mountain opened on 25 November 2026 after a five-day delay, which leaves a gap of about ten days this season. In previous seasons the gap has been wider.

Can you snowboard at Deer Valley?

No. Going into the 2026-27 season Deer Valley still lists snowboards among the devices that are not approved, and the restriction applies across the whole resort including the new expansion terrain. If anyone in your group rides, Park City Mountain is the resort to plan around.

What is new at Deer Valley for 2026-27?

The Hail Peak lift went in over the summer and opens seven new runs on the east side of the resort, reached from the Deer Valley East Village drop-off. The East Village and Park Peak lodges are reported as targeting the following winter, so the terrain is open before the buildings serving it are finished.

How long does it take to drive from Canyons Village to Deer Valley?

We have found no published door-to-door figure. Our own estimate is twenty to thirty minutes depending on traffic and which Deer Valley base you are heading for, and we would treat that as an estimate rather than a timetable.
