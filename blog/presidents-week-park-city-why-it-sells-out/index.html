Why Presidents' Week Sells Out First in Park City

Published 28 September 2026 Park City, Utah 6 min read

Every ski town has a week where the usual rules stop applying. In Park City it is the one built around Presidents' Day, and the reasons it behaves differently are worth understanding before you book.

On this page
  1. School calendars collide
  2. The mountain does not get bigger
  3. Family-sized units are genuinely scarce
  4. What it costs
  5. Skiing it well anyway

Presidents' Day is the third Monday in February. In 2027 that is Monday 15 February, and the ski week built around it runs Saturday 13 February to Saturday 20 February.

That seven night block sells out earlier and at higher rates than any other week of the season, including Christmas in some categories. Three things cause it.

School calendars collide

A large part of the north east and midwest takes the same February break. Those families cannot move their dates. When demand is inelastic and supply is fixed, price does the work, and the booking window moves earlier every year as people learn this.

The mountain does not get bigger

Lift capacity is the same in February as it is in a quiet week in January. More visitors arrive, the lifts carry the same number of people per hour, and the queues absorb the difference.

This is why where you stay changes the week more than it would otherwise. Being a short walk from the lift at opening is worth considerably more during this week than during a quiet one.

Family-sized units are genuinely scarce

Most ski resort inventory is studios and one-bedrooms, because that is what sells for the other forty-odd weeks of the year. Units that sleep six to eight in one space are a small fraction of the total.

A family of eight cannot substitute two studios for a two-bedroom without changing the holiday into something else, so the scarce category stays scarce. Those units go first, and they go earliest.

What this means in practice

If you need one unit that sleeps the whole group during Presidents' Week, you are competing for a small pool against people with the same fixed dates. Booking early is not an optimisation here, it is the whole game.

What it costs

Nightly rates for a comparable two-bedroom villa reach $800 to $1,200 and beyond on the large booking platforms during this window. The units that go at the lower end of that range are generally gone by early winter.

There is a fuller breakdown in the guide to what a Park City ski week costs for a family of eight, including the lines that do not appear in the nightly rate.

Skiing it well anyway

The week is busy and no amount of planning changes that. Two habits do most of the work. Ski the first hour, when the base lifts are still quiet. Eat lunch early or late, because the on-mountain restaurants fill on a tighter schedule than the lifts.

The third, available only if you stay close enough, is going back to the unit in the middle of the day. More in the guide to February conditions.

Common questions

When is Presidents' Week 2027?

Presidents' Day 2027 is Monday 15 February. The ski week around it runs Saturday 13 February to Saturday 20 February 2027, matching the Saturday-to-Saturday changeover most Park City lodging uses.

Is Presidents' Week busier than Christmas in Park City?

For family-sized lodging it is frequently harder to book. Christmas is busy too, but the February school break concentrates demand from families into one week with no flexibility.

How much more expensive is Presidents' Week?

Substantially. Comparable two-bedroom villas reach $800 to $1,200 a night and beyond on the large platforms during that window, against considerably less in quieter weeks.
