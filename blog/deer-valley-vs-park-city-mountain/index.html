Deer Valley or Park City Mountain: Which to Ski

Published 5 October 2026 Park City, Utah 6 min read

Park City has two major resorts, and visitors regularly assume they are interchangeable. They are not, and the differences are easy to get wrong when booking.

On this page
  1. The headline differences
  2. Which suits which trip
  3. Skiing both in one week
  4. The rest of Utah

Park City Mountain Resort and Deer Valley Resort are separate businesses in the same town. They have different passes, different terrain and quite different cultures.

The headline differences

Two resorts, same town.

The snowboarding policy is the one that catches people out, and it has been subject to change, so confirm it for your season before booking anything on the assumption.

Which suits which trip

Park City Mountain is the better default for a mixed group, a family, or anyone who wants variety and a lot of terrain on one ticket. The scale means a week does not exhaust it.

Deer Valley is the better choice for skiers who value grooming, shorter queues and service, and who do not need a snowboard to be welcome. It is generally the more expensive proposition.

Skiing both in one week

Entirely doable, and a good use of a day if the weather turns or the legs want something different. Two things to plan.

  • Lift access. They are on different pass products, so a day at the other resort is a separate ticket unless your pass covers it.
  • Transport. They are on opposite sides of town. The free bus connects them, and it takes longer than driving.
If you are staying in Canyons Village

Canyons Village is at the far end of Park City Mountain from Deer Valley, so a day over there is a proper excursion rather than a casual decision. Plan it as a day out, not as a change of mind at breakfast.

The rest of Utah

Several other large resorts sit within about an hour, which is the real argument for having a car if skiing variety is the point of the trip. For a family week staying in one village, most people never use it.

Common questions

What is the difference between Park City Mountain and Deer Valley?

They are separate resorts in the same town with different pass products. Park City Mountain is the largest resort in the United States by acreage and takes snowboarders. Deer Valley is known for grooming and service and has historically been skiers only, so check the current policy.

Can you ski both Deer Valley and Park City on one pass?

Not generally. They sit on different pass families, so a day at the other resort usually means a separate lift ticket.

How do you get between the two resorts?

They are on opposite sides of town. The free Park City bus connects them, and driving is quicker. From Canyons Village it is a full excursion rather than a short hop.
