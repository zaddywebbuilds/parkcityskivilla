The Deer Valley World Cup 2027, and How to Watch It From Canyons Village

Published 30 October 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

The Deer Valley World Cup 2027 runs from 28 to 30 January, three evenings of moguls and aerials under floodlights on courses built for the 2002 Winter Olympics. It is free to watch, which makes it the best-value ticket of the Park City winter. Two things have changed for this edition, and most roundups have both wrong.

What the Deer Valley World Cup is

This is a FIS Freestyle Ski World Cup stop: the top of the sport, on a three-day programme of moguls and aerials. Deer Valley has run elite international competition for twenty-nine seasons, on the courses built for the 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics. The venue is scheduled to host freestyle moguls and aerials again at the 2034 Winter Olympics, so you are watching on a hill with a long memory and a confirmed future.

What makes it worth planning around is the staging. Athletes compete under the lights, with aerialists throwing triple flips against a black sky. It photographs better than almost anything else in a Park City week, and costs nothing to watch.

When is the Deer Valley World Cup 2027?

28 to 30 January 2027, Thursday to Saturday. Deer Valley's own event page gives those dates, and local reporting from June 2026 gives the same three days.

Deer Valley has not published a day-by-day running order, so nobody can tell you yet which night the aerials finals land on. The resort names moguls and aerials; local coverage also lists dual moguls. If you are travelling for one discipline, confirm with the resort before booking anything non-refundable.

Two events, nine days apart, at opposite ends of town

January 2027 has a second televised competition: the Snow League brings a 22-foot halfpipe to Park City Mountain on 21 to 23 January. Different venue, different crowd. We covered where that pipe actually sits in the Snow League in Park City — not where most write-ups assume.

The name has changed, and guides have not caught up

For years this event was the Intermountain Health Freestyle International, and before that the Intermountain Healthcare Freestyle International. That is the name in most published guides, in older calendars, and in plenty of 2027 listings copied forward.

The 2027 edition is the Freestyle International presented by Stifel. Deer Valley's page carries the Stifel name in both its title and its heading, and local reporting used it from the June 2026 announcement onward. This matters for one reason: search the old name and you land on old pages, with old dates and old assumptions about admission. Search the resort, not the sponsor.

Is it free to watch?

Yes, with one new condition that is easy to miss. Deer Valley states that all events are free and open to the public. But new for this edition, entry after 5 pm requires a free digital ticket, reserved ahead and shown on your phone at the gates.

Read those two facts together: the competition is at night, so almost everyone attending a final needs a reserved ticket beforehand. It costs nothing, but turning up without one is the mistake this edition will generate.

Getting there from Canyons Village

First, the geography: Deer Valley and Park City Mountain are separate resorts with no lift connection. You cannot ski from one to the other. Anyone staying in Canyons Village is travelling to this event by road.

From Canyons Village, allow roughly twenty to thirty minutes by car in normal conditions. That is our own estimate, not a published figure, and it is the optimistic version: a January event evening, in the dark, with snow on the road, will take longer. Deer Valley had not published parking or shuttle arrangements when we wrote this.

If you were undecided about hiring a car, this event is an argument for one, and do you need a car in Park City works through that decision. Main Street sits between the two resorts and is the obvious place to eat before driving up; the Main Street guide covers it.

Where to stay for the World Cup weekend

There is a reasonable case for staying at Canyons Village and driving over. A World Cup stop is three evenings; the rest of a ski week is daytime, on snow, decided by which resort's terrain you want. Committing a whole week to Deer Valley to shorten three drives is a poor trade unless its skiing is the point. We set the two side by side in Deer Valley vs Park City Mountain.

The other half is supply. Late January is a good window on snow and crowds, but two televised competitions in one fortnight concentrate demand, and family-sized units are the thinnest part of the Park City market in any season. Deer Valley World Cup 2027: common questions

What are the Deer Valley World Cup 2027 dates?

28 to 30 January 2027, Thursday to Saturday, per Deer Valley's own event page and local reporting. The resort has hosted elite international ski competition for 29 seasons, and the dates have been stable since they were announced in June 2026.

Is the Deer Valley World Cup free to watch?

Deer Valley says all events are free and open to the public. New for 2027, entry after 5 pm requires a free digital ticket reserved in advance and shown on your phone at the gate. Paid VIP Freestyle Feast packages are sold separately.

Why is it called the Freestyle International presented by Stifel now?

The title sponsor changed. For years this was the Intermountain Health, and before that Intermountain Healthcare, Freestyle International. The 2027 edition is the Freestyle International presented by Stifel, so older guides and roundups still carry the wrong name.

Which events are contested at the Deer Valley World Cup?

Moguls and aerials, on the courses built for the 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics. Local reporting also lists dual moguls across the three days. Deer Valley has not published a day-by-day running order, so check the resort before planning around one discipline.

How do you get to Deer Valley from Canyons Village?

By road. Deer Valley and Park City Mountain are separate resorts with no lift connection, so there is no skiing between them. Allow roughly twenty to thirty minutes by car in normal conditions and considerably longer arriving for a night final.

Is it better to stay at Deer Valley or at Canyons Village for the World Cup?

If the competition is three evenings of a ski week, Canyons Village works well and you drive over for the finals. Staying at Deer Valley itself puts you at the venue but commits your whole week to one resort's terrain and its rates.
