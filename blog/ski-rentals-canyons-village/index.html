Ski and Snowboard Rentals in Canyons Village

Published 1 October 2026 Park City, Utah 5 min read

Rental equipment is a smaller line than lodging or lift tickets, and it causes a disproportionate amount of the friction in a ski week. Most of that is avoidable.

On this page
  1. Renting in the village
  2. Delivery services
  3. Book ahead
  4. Buying boots instead
  5. Helmets

You have three realistic options in Canyons Village. Rent in the village, rent in town and carry the kit out, or have equipment delivered to where you are staying.

Renting in the village

Convenient, and priced accordingly. The real advantage is not the shop, it is what comes with it. Many village rental operations include overnight storage at the base of the lift, which is the part that changes your mornings.

If you are comparing on daily rate alone you will pick wrong. A slightly cheaper shop that leaves you carrying eight sets of skis and boots back to the unit every evening is not cheaper.

Delivery services

Several operators will bring equipment to your accommodation, fit everybody there, and collect at the end of the week. For a large group, particularly one with children, this is worth pricing.

Being fitted in your own living room rather than in a queue on the first afternoon of a holiday week is a genuinely better experience, and the price is often comparable.

Book ahead

During Presidents' Week the rental shops are as busy as everything else. Booking in advance is cheaper and it means your sizes are reserved, which matters most for children and for anyone needing less common boot sizes.

What to book before you fly

Rentals, ski school, lift tickets and a spa treatment if you want one. All four are capacity-limited during a holiday week, and all four are cheaper or better when reserved rather than arranged on arrival.

Buying boots instead

If you ski more than about a week a year, boots are the one piece of equipment worth owning. Rental boots are the usual cause of a bad day, and fit is personal in a way that skis are not.

Skis and boards are a different calculation. Renting lets you match the kit to the conditions, and it means you are not paying airline fees to bring your own.

Helmets

Include them in the rental booking rather than buying on arrival, and check whether they are bundled or priced separately. For children they are not optional, and the ski school will expect them.

Common questions

Can you rent skis in Canyons Village?

Yes, there are rental operations in the village, including on site at the resorts. Many include overnight ski storage at the base of the lift, which matters more day to day than the rental rate.

Should I book ski rentals in advance?

Yes for a holiday week. Booking ahead is usually cheaper and reserves your sizes, which matters most for children and less common boot sizes.

Is ski delivery worth it in Park City?

For a large group or a family with children, often yes. Being fitted at your accommodation rather than queuing in a shop on the first afternoon is a better experience and the price is frequently comparable.
