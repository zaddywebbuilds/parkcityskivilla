Christmas Week in Park City 2026: The Honest Booking Picture

Published 24 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Christmas in Park City 2026 is largely gone. Lodging sources describe the holiday week as booking six to twelve months in advance, and by late November those reservations are in place. This guide is written for two groups: anyone still hunting a last-minute opening, and anyone who has just realised they need to plan further ahead than they thought.

Why Christmas week fills this fast

The demand for Park City lodging at Christmas is not about the skiing on those particular days. It is about school calendars being fixed and family traditions being sticky. A group that skied Canyons Village over Christmas three years ago will generally book the same week for the same period, often before they have finished the previous season. Lodging sources consistently describe the period from 26 December through 2 January as the first week to fill each autumn, ahead of Presidents’ Week, ahead of spring break, and well ahead of anything in January.

The result is that searching for Christmas 2026 accommodation in late November means hunting in a market built from other people’s cancellations. There is a gap between that reality and what most lodging guides say, partly because the people writing them have rooms to sell. How far ahead Park City’s peak weeks book covers the full calendar if you are planning for next season.

The Epic Local blackout: 26–31 December is a restricted week

Here is the detail competitor guides consistently miss. Epic Local Pass holders cannot use their pass at Park City Mountain on any day from 26 to 31 December 2026. The blackout is confirmed on the Epic Pass restricted-dates page for the 2026–27 season, with Park City on the restricted-resort list. The full Epic Pass has no blackout on those dates.

What that means in practice: anyone arriving with an Epic Local Pass for Christmas week either needs to upgrade to a full Epic or buy day tickets for every day they ski. Day tickets at Park City Mountain are priced dynamically, and what lift access costs for a week works through those numbers in detail. Peak online prices have been reported at roughly $325 per day, with walk-up rates higher still. For a family of four skiing six days, that is a material line in the holiday budget that the pass price alone does not cover.

Epic Local holders may be able to buy a half-price restricted-day ticket as an alternative. Pass guides describe this option as available, but confirm it on epicpass.com before travelling, because the product page does not list it explicitly.

What is on the mountain at Christmas

Park City Mountain has run a torchlight parade on Christmas Eve for more than sixty years. In recent seasons the event has been held at Canyons Village, with a ski and ride school team descent, live music and complimentary hot drinks at the village base. The dates and running order for the 2026–27 season had not been published at the time this was written; check parkcitymountain.com or cvma.com once the holiday calendar appears.

Beyond the parade, the village itself is fully operational by Christmas in a way it is not at the opening weeks of the season. What was open at opening weekend shows the contrast: at the end of November, The Farm had not yet opened and the new parking garage was not yet accepting cars. By Christmas, both are running. The Farm, Red Tail Grill and the other Canyons Village restaurants are all in service, the village is decorated and the plaza buzzes through the week.

If you are still looking: how cancellations surface

Cancellations at Christmas exist, but they are not predictable and they are rarely cheap. The most reliable method is to monitor the same booking channels you would use to book in the first place, with notifications set for the specific dates and unit sizes you need. Direct-owner rentals sometimes hold availability later than platform listings, because the booking window and minimum-stay requirements are set by the owner rather than by an algorithm.

What matters most if you are hunting: flexibility on check-in and check-out dates, willingness to take a unit that is not your first choice of size or layout, and a clear sense of must-have versus nice-to-have. Resort fees and what the all-in cost actually looks like is worth reading before any late booking, because a quoted nightly rate and the total paid can differ by more than expected at this time of year.

The move that actually works: booking 2027 now

The audience this guide is most useful to is not people who need a room in four weeks. It is people who are standing in late November 2026, have just realised Christmas has gone, and are wondering when to act for next year.

The answer is now. Presidents’ Week 2027 runs 13–20 February. At the time this was written, that week still had openings available through direct-owner rentals. The Epic Pass sales deadline for the 2026–27 season was 6 December 2026, so anyone needing a pass for a February trip needed it in hand before that date. Can you still book Park City lodging for Christmas 2026?

Most lodging at Canyons Village and across Park City is already committed for Christmas week. Cancellations do surface, but finding one requires flexibility on dates, unit size and price.

How does Christmas week compare to Presidents’ Week at Park City?

Both are peak demand, but Presidents’ Week books on a slightly longer lead than Christmas in some years. Epic Local blackout days at Presidents’ Week are only 13 and 14 February, not the whole week. Full terrain is typically open by February, while Christmas week lands during a period when terrain coverage is still building through the month of December.
