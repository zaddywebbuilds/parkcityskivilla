Canyons Village Ski Week Cost in 2027: What You Are Actually Paying

Published 26 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Skiers planning a week at Canyons Village in 2027 often ask for a single number. There is not one. What a ski week costs depends on seven separate lines, two of which — resort fees and parking — rarely appear in the headline rate. Here is what each line is and where it ranges.

A useful starting point is to lay out every cost category before drilling into any one of them. The table below gives each line, what drives it, and a short note on where it typically lands. The most variable line — lodging — is intentionally left without a figure: the right approach is to request an all-in quote that includes the resort fee, so the comparison is apples to apples. Lift access: passes versus day tickets in 2026–27

Lift access is the second-largest line in most ski week budgets and the one with the most decision points. For the 2026–27 season at Park City Mountain Resort, the main options are the Epic Pass, the Epic Local Pass, the Epic Day Pass, and single-day lift tickets. Vail Resorts published the following prices in November 2026: the full Epic Pass is $1,245 for adults and $995 for passholders aged 13 to 30. The Epic Local Pass, which carries blackout dates on the busiest weeks, is $959 for adults and $759 for ages 13 to 30. Those prices apply only until the sales window closes on 6 December 2026. After that date, day tickets are the only option for the 2026–27 season.

Single-day tickets purchased at the window on peak days are priced dynamically and are significantly higher than the advance online rate. The lift ticket cost guide for 2026–27 tracks the full pricing picture including blackout dates by pass tier.

Ski and snowboard rentals at Canyons Village

Three rental shops operate at or near Canyons Village. Canyon Mountain Sports is the main on-mountain option, located at the base of the Gondola. Aloha Ski and Board operates near the Westgate base area. Epic Mountain Rentals runs out of Silverado Lodge and is the Epic brand’s own shop; Epic Pass holders receive a 20 percent discount on rentals there. All three offer a range of packages from recreational to performance, with multi-day rates that reduce the per-day cost the longer the rental runs.

Advance online booking is consistently cheaper than counter rates, particularly on peak weeks. The ski rentals guide for Canyons Village gives current pricing by shop and package tier.

Getting here from Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City International Airport is the standard arrival point for Canyons Village. The drive in good conditions takes roughly 45 to 60 minutes via Interstate 80 east to the Park City exit. During peak weeks, the canyon roads can slow significantly in poor weather or on busy weekend afternoons. Private shuttle and car services quote by vehicle size and by the number of ski bags; always give both numbers when requesting a rate, since ski bag fees can be a material part of the total for a large group.

Once on property, no car is needed; the ski-in access from the villa, free Park City Transit from Kimball Junction, and walkable groceries at the junction cover all daily needs. The Salt Lake City to Park City transfer guide covers the main providers and what to include in a quote.

Groceries versus restaurants: the kitchen advantage

A full kitchen changes the food budget for a week significantly. Groups and families who self-cater breakfast and at least some dinners report total food spend well below the all-restaurant alternative. The villa has a full kitchen; the full-kitchen cooking guide covers what to stock on arrival day.

Smith’s at Kimball Junction, about three miles from Canyons Village, carries a full grocery range; Whole Foods in the same area covers specialty items. Neither sells wine or spirits — Utah grocery stores are limited to beer at or below 5 percent ABV — so plan a separate stop at the Park City state liquor store (1500 Kearns Boulevard), which is closed on Sundays and state and federal holidays.

The costs that do not appear in the headline price

Resort fees are the most common budget surprise at ski properties. When comparing accommodation, ask for the full nightly total including all mandatory fees, not the room rate alone; the difference over seven nights can be significant.

Canyons Village garage parking carries a daily fee; groups arriving by private transfer and using the free transit throughout the stay avoid this line entirely. Tips at ski valet, restaurants, and shuttle drivers are a further incidental worth including in any budget from the start. The resort fees and hidden costs guide covers what to request and what to watch for in the booking process.

The two-bedroom villa at Westgate Park City Resort sleeps eight, with a full kitchen, two bathrooms, and ski-in, ski-out access to Canyons Village. Available weeks for the 2026–27 season are Presidents’ Week, 13–20 February 2027, and spring break, 21–28 March 2027.

Common questions

What is the cheapest way to ski Park City Mountain for a week in 2027?

The Epic Pass at $1,245 adult (or $995 for ages 13 to 30) is the lowest per-day cost for a full week if purchased before the December 6, 2026 sales close. After that date, the Epic Day Pass purchased in advance online covers days one through seven at $50 to $106 per day, putting a seven-day total at a maximum of around $742 at the highest rate. Single-day tickets purchased at the window on peak days are priced dynamically and are significantly more expensive than advance online purchase.

How much do ski rentals cost at Canyons Village?

Rental pricing varies by shop, package tier, and number of days. The on-mountain options are Canyon Mountain Sports, Aloha Ski and Board near the Westgate base, and Epic Mountain Rentals at Silverado Lodge. Epic Pass holders receive 20 percent off at Epic Mountain Rentals locations. All three shops offer lower prices for advance online booking than counter rates on the day, so reserving before you arrive is the standard way to reduce this line item.

Do I need a car once I arrive at Canyons Village?

No. Once at Canyons Village, the ski-in, ski-out access to Park City Mountain Resort means no vehicle is needed for skiing. Free Park City Transit runs from Kimball Junction and covers the resort area throughout the day during ski season, connecting to the grocery stores at Kimball Junction and to Main Street. The main use case for a car is arriving from and departing to Salt Lake City International Airport.

What resort fees should I expect at Westgate Park City?

Resort fee details are property-specific and can change seasonally. When requesting a quote, ask for the full all-in nightly rate that includes the resort fee, parking, and any other mandatory charges, rather than the room rate alone. The difference between the quoted room rate and the all-in total can be significant over a full week, and some guests are surprised by charges that did not appear in the initial rate.

Is a full Epic Pass worth buying for a one-week trip to Park City?

For a seven-day trip, the Epic Day Pass at $50 to $106 per day in advance puts the total at around $742 maximum, below the Epic Pass at $1,245 adult but without multi-resort value. The Epic Local Pass at $959 is a middle option but carries blackout dates on the busiest weeks, including most of Christmas week.
