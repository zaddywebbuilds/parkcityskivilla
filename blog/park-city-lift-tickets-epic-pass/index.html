Park City Lift Tickets and the Epic Pass: Doing the Maths

Published 1 October 2026 Park City, Utah 6 min read

Lift access is the second largest cost of a ski week and the one where the gap between planning ahead and turning up is widest.

Park City Mountain Resort is an Epic Pass mountain, which gives you three broad options. Buy day tickets at the window, buy multi-day tickets in advance, or buy a pass product.

Prices move every season and by date, so the numbers below are about method rather than figures. Check current pricing before you commit.

The window is the expensive option

Walk-up day tickets at a major American resort during a holiday week are the highest price available, by a wide margin. This is deliberate and it is consistent across the industry.

Buying the same days online in advance is meaningfully cheaper, and the discount generally grows the further ahead you buy. If your dates are fixed, there is no reason to wait.

When a pass beats day tickets

The crossover arrives sooner than people expect, especially for a family. A season pass for a week of skiing sounds absurd until you price seven peak-week day tickets for the same person.

Run the comparison properly.

  1. Price your exact days, in advance, for each person, at holiday-week rates.
  2. Price the relevant pass products for each person, including any child or youth pricing.
  3. Add any extra value you would actually use, such as days at other resorts on the same pass during the trip.
  4. Compare the totals, not the per-day figures.
The family multiplier

Every lift-access decision multiplies by the number of people. A $40 per person per day difference is $40 on a solo trip and well over $2,000 across a family of eight for a week. This is the line where planning ahead pays most.

Children, and who skis for free

Age bands for child, youth and senior pricing vary by product and change between seasons. For a large family these bands move the total substantially, so check which ones your group falls into rather than assuming.

Lessons include lift access, sometimes

Children's ski school packages often bundle a lift ticket. If you are buying lessons anyway, check what is included before you separately buy tickets for the same days. Families buy the same access twice more often than you would think.

More on booking lessons early in the family guide.

Collecting passes

The Canyons base ticket office sits at the bottom of the Red Pine Gondola, so if you are staying in the village this is a walk rather than a drive. Do it the afternoon you arrive rather than on the first ski morning, when everybody else is doing it.

Common questions

Is Park City on the Epic Pass?

Yes. Park City Mountain Resort is an Epic Pass mountain, so pass products are an alternative to buying day or multi-day lift tickets.

Are lift tickets cheaper bought in advance?

Yes, generally by a wide margin compared with walk-up window prices, and the saving tends to grow the further ahead you buy. During a holiday week the difference is at its largest.

When is a season pass cheaper than day tickets?

Sooner than most people expect for a full week at peak rates, especially across a family. Price your exact days in advance against the pass products for each person and compare the totals.
