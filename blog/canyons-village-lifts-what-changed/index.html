The Canyons Village Cabriolet Is Gone: Lift Guide for 2026-27

Published 17 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Search for a Canyons Village guide and you will likely find at least one that tells you to look for the Cabriolet. The Cabriolet made its last run on 29 March 2026 and was removed when the season ended. This guide says what replaced it, what actually changed, and what did not.

Which guides are still getting it wrong

One guide in the top results for Canyons Village on major search engines runs to 932 words and still names “the Canyons Cabriolet” as the way to reach the village from the parking area. It was written well before the lift stopped operating and has not been updated. The same guide mentions neither the Skyway that replaced it, nor the Sunrise Gondola, nor the Orange Bubble Express. It is not an outlier; a search for Canyons Village lifts in mid-November 2026 returns several guides still describing the Cabriolet as if it were running.

This matters because the Cabriolet and the Skyway serve the same purpose but differ in location, cabin design, boarding logistics, and the way the parking area is laid out. Arriving with the wrong information costs time on a morning when every minute before the lifts open counts. It also misleads non-skiers and partners waiting at the village base, who need to know which terminal they are heading for.

What the Cabriolet actually was

The Cabriolet had operated at Canyons Village for roughly three decades before its final run on 29 March 2026, one week before the end of the 2025-26 season. It was an open-air gondola — no seats, no cover, no glass. You stood in a small car that ran on a cable up the hillside from the parking area to the village base. Standing in an open car at six in the morning in a February that runs to minus fifteen is cold in a way that takes some explaining to anyone who has only ever read about it.

It was free, it was a fixture, and it was genuinely useful over those decades. Several generations of visitors put it into their guides and have not been back to check. The final service was ceremonial; local press covered the last day. The lift has since been removed and the parking structure that now connects to the Skyway occupies part of the same footprint.

What replaced it: the Canyons Village Skyway

The Canyons Village Skyway opened for the 2026-27 season, following roughly the same route from the parking area to the village base. The key differences are in the cabin, the capacity, and an added mid-station.

The Skyway uses ten-person enclosed cabins with floor-to-ceiling glass rather than the open standing car of the Cabriolet. It is free to ride, as the Cabriolet was. It runs from 7am to 6pm. A mid-station was added in the mid-village area, which the Cabriolet did not have.

The Skyway spans roughly 2,700 feet and rises about 200 feet from the lower station to the upper terminal at the Forum. It runs continuously rather than on a timetable, so there is no schedule to catch.

Village transport, not a ski lift: why it matters

Both the Cabriolet and the Skyway are village transport systems, not ski lifts, and that is a distinction worth being direct about because several guides describe the Skyway as a lift and imply that a pass is required.

The Skyway runs below the ski terrain, connecting the parking structure to the village base. It does not require a lift ticket or an Epic Pass. Anyone — skiers, snowboarders, non-skiers, families waiting in the base area, resort employees — can board it free, without any pass check. This is what the Cabriolet also was.

The ski lifts — Red Pine Gondola, Orange Bubble Express, Sunrise Gondola, Tombstone Express — start from the village base, above where the Skyway drops you, and climb into the ski terrain. Those require a valid pass or day ticket. The Canyons Village lift map guide covers which lift to take for which part of the mountain and what the connections look like.

The confusion arises because the Skyway is a gondola and gondolas elsewhere are often ski lifts. Here it is not. It ends at the base, not on the mountain.

The ski lifts that actually put you on the mountain

From the village base, the main ski lifts that climb into the terrain are:

Red Pine Gondola — an eight-person gondola, the primary route up from the village, climbing to the Red Pine Lodge area where you connect to the top of the resort and to the Quicksilver Gondola linking the Canyons side to the Park City town side.

Orange Bubble Express — a four-person hooded, heated high-speed quad. One of the first lifts to load in the morning and one of the fastest ways to gain elevation from the village base.

Sunrise Gondola — a ten-person gondola running to the Red Pine area, opened in the 2024-25 season. Some stale guides also omit this one.

Tombstone Express — a six-person high-speed chair linking terrain in the mid-mountain area above the village.

Lifts at Canyons Village operate 9am to 4pm as the resort-wide window, with individual lifts opening on their own schedule within that. The early-season guide covers which lifts spin first in November when the opening package is thin.

What this means for arriving in 2026-27

When you arrive by car at Canyons Village this season, you will board the Skyway from the new parking structure off Frostwood Drive. The old Cabriolet lot — the surface area it used to serve — is part of the new garage construction footprint. If you have directions from an older guide, they will be describing a location that has changed.

For opening weekend specifically, note that the new parking garage is not scheduled to open until early December. The first few weeks of the season have different parking arrangements; check the resort’s current parking page before you drive up.

Common questions

Is the Cabriolet still running at Canyons Village?

No. The Canyons Cabriolet made its final run on 29 March 2026 and was removed when the season ended. It operated as an open-air standing gondola for roughly three decades before being retired. It is not closed for the season or under refurbishment; it is gone permanently.

What replaced the Cabriolet gondola at Canyons Village?

The Canyons Village Skyway, a ten-person enclosed gondola running from the new parking structure to the village base. It follows roughly the same route as the Cabriolet and adds a mid-station in the mid-village area. Like the Cabriolet, it is free to ride. It runs from 7am to 6pm on the 2026-27 season schedule.

Is the Canyons Village Skyway free to ride?

Yes. The Skyway is village transport and carries no ticket or lift pass requirement. Anyone can board it, including non-skiers and guests who are not skiing that day. It runs continuously from 7am to 6pm during the ski season, same as the Cabriolet was free in its time.

What is the difference between the Skyway and the ski lifts at Canyons Village?

The Skyway runs below the ski terrain, connecting the parking structure to the village base. It does not require a lift pass. The ski lifts — Red Pine Gondola, Orange Bubble Express, Sunrise Gondola, Tombstone Express — start from the village base and climb into the ski terrain. Those require a valid pass or day ticket.

Where do I board the Canyons Village Skyway?

The lower station sits at the new Canyons Village Parking Structure off Frostwood Drive. A mid-station serves the mid-village area. The upper station delivers passengers to the Canyons Village Forum, at the base of the main ski lifts. The Skyway runs continuously from 7am to 6pm during the ski season.
