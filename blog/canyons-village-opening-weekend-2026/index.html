Canyons Village Opening Weekend 2026: A Practical Guide to What Is and Is Not There Yet

Published 17 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Opening day for the 2026-27 Park City Mountain season is Wednesday 25 November, five days later than the date held since August. If you are staying at Canyons Village for opening weekend, the village itself does not fully open with the mountain. This guide covers what is running, what is not yet open, and what that means for a November stay.

Why opening day moved to 25 November

The resort held Friday 20 November as its target from August until the middle of the month, then pushed it back citing snow conditions — it wanted weather that would let crews build a surface worth skiing rather than open on the thinnest cover it could get away with.

That weather arrived. Storms dropped 38 inches over several days, with cold temperatures that gave the snowmaking crews their strongest stretch of the autumn, and on Sunday 22 November the resort confirmed Wednesday 25 November. A five-day slip is mild: last season the target was 21 November and the lifts did not turn until 5 December.

What the new date does to the weekend

Opening day is now the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, so the village fills on a holiday week rather than a quiet one. It also means 27 and 28 November are peak restricted dates on which the Epic Local Pass cannot be used at Park City — the Friday and Saturday of the resort’s own opening weekend. The full Epic Pass is unrestricted. The arithmetic is in what a week of lift access costs.

Parking on opening weekend: the garage is not open yet

The new Canyons Village Parking Structure is scheduled to open in early December, not on opening day. This matters because most of what has been written about Canyons Village parking describes the new setup: a $29-per-day structure with the Skyway gondola attached. That will be true from December, but not on 25 November.

Until the garage opens, parking in the Canyons Village surface lot is free and first-come, first-served. Spaces are limited. When the garage does open, the daily rate is $29, with free parking after midday and free carpool spots for vehicles with four or more occupants. Those terms apply only once the structure is operational. Check the resort’s current parking page before you go.

The Canyons Village Skyway will be running regardless — free to ride, 7am to 6pm — but the lower boarding station at the base of the new parking structure is part of the December build-out. In early season, boarding is from the existing village-side station.

What is not open yet — and when it opens

The Farm is the marquee dining venue in Canyons Village, sitting at the base of the Red Pine Gondola in the Forum complex. It is not open on opening weekend. The hours listings show it opening on 4 December. If your plan is to ski, then eat at The Farm, then ski again, that is a mid-December plan rather than a November one.

The parking garage also does not come online until early December. The full Canyons Village — with the garage operational, The Farm serving, and the full range of Forum retail running — builds up over the first two weeks of the season, not in a single day.

Getting gear: ski rentals on opening weekend

Canyon Mountain Rentals in the Forum is listed as opening with the mountain. If you are hiring skis or a snowboard for the week, do not leave this for the morning queue on opening day. Lines at rental counters on the first day of a season are long at every resort, and opening day at a resort the size of Park City Mountain draws a crowd even when the terrain is thin.

The practical move is to book equipment in advance rather than collecting it on the morning. The ski rentals at Canyons Village guide covers what to book ahead, what the shop at the Forum carries, and whether performance equipment is worth the upgrade in November. For anyone arriving on the Thursday before opening day, picking up gear that afternoon avoids the morning rush entirely.

If you are bringing your own skis, bring rock skis rather than your best pair. The terrain in mid-November is thin, the groomed surface has rocks close to the margins, and anything off the marked run is a hazard.

Common questions

What is open in Canyons Village on opening day 2026?

The resort's hours listings show Canyon Mountain Rentals and Canyon Mountain Sports in the Forum both opening with the mountain on 25 November, along with the Skyway gondola from 7am to 6pm. Red Pine Lodge at the top of the gondola opens for dining from 10:30am to 3:30pm. Hours can change; confirm at parkcitymountain.com before you go.

Is the new Canyons Village parking garage open on opening weekend?

No. The new parking structure is scheduled to open in early December 2026, not on opening day. Until then, the Canyons Village surface lot is free and first-come, first-served. When the garage does open, the daily rate is $29, with free parking after midday and free carpool spots for vehicles with four or more occupants.

Where do I eat in Canyons Village on opening weekend?

Red Pine Lodge at the top of the Red Pine Gondola serves lunch from about 10:30am to 3:30pm, so it is accessible only once you have a lift ticket and have ridden up. At the village base, options in the Forum are more limited on opening weekend; The Farm does not open until 4 December. Bring snacks for the morning.

Can I rent ski equipment at Canyons Village on opening day?

Yes. Canyon Mountain Rentals in the Forum is listed as opening with the mountain on 25 November. If you are hiring equipment for the week, consider booking ahead rather than queuing on opening morning. Opening day lines at rental counters run long at every resort, and that is especially true at a resort the size of Park City Mountain.

Is it worth visiting Canyons Village on opening weekend?

Only if being there at the start of the season is what you want. The terrain is thin, the groomed runs are few, the village is not yet fully operational, and the garage is not yet open. If the skiing itself is the priority, a week in February or late March returns a fully open mountain for a comparable lodging rate.
