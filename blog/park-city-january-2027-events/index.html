Park City Events in January 2027, and the First January Without Sundance

Published 30 October 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Anyone planning Park City January 2027 events needs to know one thing first, and it is not on most calendars yet: Sundance has gone. After more than forty years, the festival moves to Boulder, Colorado for its 2027 edition, and January in Park City becomes a different month. What fills the gap is skiing, and two televised competitions nine days apart.

Sundance has left Park City

In March 2025 the Sundance Institute's board announced that Boulder, Colorado would become the festival's home beginning in 2027. The 2026 edition, which ran in Park City and Salt Lake City from 22 January to 1 February 2026, was the last in Utah. The festival had been here for more than forty years and celebrated its 41st edition in 2025.

Sundance 2027 runs in Boulder from 21 to 31 January 2027 — which means it overlaps the Snow League almost exactly. For forty years the third week of January in Park City belonged to a film festival. In 2027 that same week belongs to a halfpipe competition.

Be careful how much you read into this for lodging. Sundance demand was always concentrated in town: Main Street venues, the theatres, the hotels within walking distance. Canyons Village, at the far end of the resort, was never the festival's centre of gravity. The departure of a very large non-skiing crowd changes the town side of Park City more than it changes the ski villages.

What that was worth

For scale: Boulder's tourism board, citing the festival's own figures, puts the 2025 Sundance contribution at $196.1 million to Utah's GDP. Neither the Institute's announcement nor Boulder's own material confirms any continuing Utah festival programming after 2026, so treat January 2027 as a clean break rather than a partial move.

The Snow League, 21 to 23 January

Shaun White's competition series brings a 22-foot halfpipe to Park City Mountain for three days, Thursday to Saturday, the first pipe that size here since the 2019 FIS World Championships. General admission starts at $10, rising to $25 for the Saturday final, and it streams on Peacock and the league's YouTube channel.

The detail that catches people out is the venue. The pipe is at the Park City town base, not Canyons Village — the two bases are one resort but fifteen minutes apart by road, joined only over the top. We laid out where it sits in the Snow League in Park City. The dates also moved: an earlier announcement said 22 to 24 January and that range is still circulating.

The Deer Valley World Cup, 28 to 30 January

A week later, moguls and aerials under floodlights at Deer Valley, on courses built for the 2002 Winter Olympics. This one is free, which makes it the best value of the month, but there is a new catch: entry after 5 pm requires a free digital ticket reserved in advance. The competition is at night, so in practice that applies to nearly everyone.

It is also no longer called what most guides call it. The title sponsor changed and the 2027 edition is the Freestyle International presented by Stifel, not the Intermountain Health event of previous years. Our guide to the Deer Valley World Cup covers the access, the ticket rule and the drive from Canyons Village.

What is on in Canyons Village all month

Neither competition is in Canyons Village, but the village runs its own programme, and it is free. Après at the Airstream sets up at the Forum by the Umbrella Bar most weekends from January to April, Friday and Sunday afternoons and Saturday lunchtime. There is a pub quiz on Friday evenings. The Epic passholder party lands at Red Pine Lodge on 29 January, in the village itself rather than at either competition venue.

That is the quiet argument for staying on this side in January: the events you can walk to are here, and the ones you drive to are a manageable evening out. Après-ski in Canyons Village covers where everybody goes at four.

Which January weeks book out, and which do not

January 2027 splits unusually cleanly. The last third of the month is now the busy part: two televised competitions and a passholder party between the 21st and the 30th, where previously the same stretch was Sundance. The crowd changes but the demand on beds does not vanish.

Mid-January, roughly the 10th to the 20th, is the gap. A deeper base than December, colder and drier snow than March, shorter queues than Presidents' Week, and now no festival sitting on top of it. If you want the best value week of the Park City winter, that is where to look. Park City January 2027: common questions

Is Sundance still in Park City in January 2027?

No. The Sundance Institute announced in March 2025 that Boulder, Colorado would become the festival's home beginning in 2027. The 2026 edition, in Park City and Salt Lake City, was the last in Utah after more than forty years.

What are the main Park City events in January 2027?

Two televised ski competitions. The Snow League brings a 22-foot halfpipe to Park City Mountain on 21 to 23 January, and the Freestyle International World Cup runs at Deer Valley on 28 to 30 January. The Epic passholder party falls on 29 January.

Are the January 2027 events free to attend?

The Deer Valley World Cup is free, though entry after 5 pm now needs a free digital ticket reserved ahead. The Snow League is ticketed, with general admission listed from 10 dollars and a 25 dollar Saturday final.

Which January 2027 week is busiest in Park City?

The last third of the month, which now carries both competitions and the Epic passholder party between 21 and 30 January. Mid-January, roughly the 10th to the 20th, is the quieter stretch and the better value.

Is January a good time to ski Park City?

It is one of the better windows of the season: a deeper base than December, colder and drier snow than March, and shorter lift queues than Presidents' Week. The trade is cold mornings and the shortest daylight of the ski season.

What is on at the Egyptian Theatre in January 2027?

We could not verify a January 2027 programme. The listings we found were ticket aggregators covering February onwards, and they disagreed with each other on dates. Check the theatre's own box office rather than a resale listing.
