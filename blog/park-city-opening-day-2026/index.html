Park City Opening Day 2026: What Is Open on 25 November

Published 16 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Park City opening day 2026 is Wednesday 25 November, not the 20 November the resort had been targeting since August. The date slipped, then 38 inches of snow arrived and settled it. Six of the nine lifts turning on day one are on the Canyons Village side.

Most guides to opening day were written in September, named 20 November, and have not been touched since. That date is now wrong, and the lift list below is published, so this page can tell you what is actually open rather than what somebody hoped would be.

Why the date moved to 25 November

Vail Resorts named Friday 20 November when it announced the season in August, and local reporting still carried that date into the middle of the month. The resort then pushed it back, citing snow conditions: it was waiting for weather that would let crews build a surface worth skiing rather than open on the thinnest possible cover.

That weather arrived. Storms dropped 38 inches over several days, and the cold that came with them gave the snowmaking crews their best stretch of the autumn. On Sunday 22 November the resort confirmed Wednesday 25 November, and the chief operating officer told staff that opening day was officially set.

A five-day slip is mild by recent standards. Last season the resort originally aimed at 21 November and did not open until 5 December, because the snow simply did not come. Every announcement still carries the same condition, weather and conditions dependent, and that is not boilerplate.

Which lifts and runs are open on opening day

This is the part nobody else has, because it was only published three days before the date. Nine lifts are listed for day one, and the split between the two base areas matters a great deal if you are staying in Canyons Village.

Read that table twice if you are choosing where to stay. Six of the nine lifts are at Canyons Village, including both gondolas onto the mountain. The snowmaking aimed at terrain off Saddleback and High Meadow through early November delivered exactly what it pointed at, and both lifts are open on day one. Canyons Village is usually treated as the second string in early season because the town base carries the history; on 25 November it is the better side to be standing on, a short walk from the villa floor plan.

Why the Skyway matters more in November than in February

The Canyons Village Skyway is on the opening-day list, and 25 November is its first day carrying skiers and snowboarders this season. It is not a ski lift. It is enclosed village transport running from the parking area up to the village base, free to ride, in ten-person cabins with glass sides, and it replaced the open standing Cabriolet that ran here for three decades.

In February that is a convenience. In November it is the difference between a pleasant start and a cold one, because you will spend longer standing about at the base on a day when two runs are open. The full story is in the Cabriolet lift guide.

What early-season cover actually means at Canyons Village

Two named runs at this base, both served by lifts that climb into the Red Pine area. That is a morning of laps, not a day of exploring, and it will be busy because everybody who has waited five extra days is going at once.

The surface will be mostly manufactured snow under whatever the storms left. It skis firm and fast early and heavier by afternoon, with rocks and stumps close to the surface at the edges, which is why regulars put old skis on. Stay on the groomed, marked runs.

What you will not get is the thing the resort is famous for. More than 330 trails and over 40 lifts across 7,300 acres is the full-season figure, and on opening day you are skiing a rounding error of it — see what November really gives you.

Booking around opening week

One thing to know before you count on a pass. Opening day is the Wednesday, Thanksgiving is the Thursday, and 27 and 28 November are peak restricted dates on which the Epic Local Pass cannot be used at Park City. The Friday and Saturday of the resort's own opening weekend are blacked out for Epic Local holders. The full Epic Pass is unrestricted. If the skiing rather than the occasion is the point, the honest advice is to look past November. A week in February or late March buys a mountain that is open, a base that is deep and ski school running at full strength. That trade-off is in how far ahead to book Presidents Week.

The practical version

Two runs at this base on day one, six of nine lifts on this side, 38 inches in the bank and a blackout on the Friday and Saturday if you hold Epic Local. Bring rock skis, take the Skyway up, and come back in February for the actual skiing — see the weeks we have open.

Common questions

When does Park City Mountain open for the 2026-27 season?

Wednesday 25 November 2026. The resort had targeted Friday 20 November since August, delayed it citing snow conditions, then confirmed the new date on Sunday 22 November after storms dropped 38 inches. The season is planned to run to 19 April 2027.

Why did Park City Mountain delay its opening day?

The resort pushed the 20 November target back while waiting for weather that would let crews build a quality snow surface rather than open on minimal cover. The storms that followed brought 38 inches and the cold temperatures that came with them gave snowmaking crews their strongest stretch of the autumn.

Which lifts are open at Canyons Village on opening day?

Six of the nine lifts listed for 25 November are at Canyons Village: the Canyons Village Skyway, the Red Pine Gondola, the Sunrise Gondola, Saddleback, High Meadow and the Hot Shot Carpet. They serve two named runs, Mellow Moose and Kokopelli. Early-season terrain changes daily, so confirm on the resort snow report.

Can I use an Epic Local Pass at Park City on opening weekend?

Not on the Friday or the Saturday. Epic lists 27 and 28 November 2026 as peak restricted dates on which the Epic Local Pass cannot be used at Park City, which is the Friday and Saturday of opening weekend. Opening day itself, Wednesday 25 November, and Thanksgiving Thursday are not restricted. The full Epic Pass has no blackout dates.

Is it worth skiing Park City on opening weekend?

Only if the occasion is the point. Expect a handful of groomed runs on mostly manufactured snow, with rocks and stumps close to the surface and crowds concentrated on very little terrain. If you want the mountain open and a deep base, February or late March is a far better week for the money.
