Renting Park City Villas for a Large Group of up to 24

Published 4 November 2026 Park City, Utah 7 min read

Finding a Park City ski rental for a large group usually means one of two bad outcomes: a single enormous house a long drive from the lifts, or a scatter of hotel rooms with nowhere for twenty people to be together. There is a third shape, and it is the one most groups of this size actually want.

What three villas in one resort give you

We own three Premier Two-Bedroom Villas at Westgate Park City Resort, in Canyons Village. Each sleeps eight. Together they take a group of up to 24 — in the same resort, a short walk from the Red Pine Gondola, sharing the same pools, the same ski storage and the same front door onto the village.

The number that matters is six bathrooms. A large rental house advertised for twenty-four frequently has three or four, and on a ski morning when everyone is trying to leave at once, that is the constraint people remember.

How a group of 24 actually divides

Split by household, not by headcount. Each villa is a self-contained eight: two bedrooms, two living areas, two bathrooms and its own front door. So the natural unit is a family of six to eight, or two couples with children, taking one villa outright.

What to avoid is splitting one family across two units. It sounds efficient on a spreadsheet and it is miserable in practice, because somebody ends up walking between buildings at bedtime. If a group of 24 is really three families of eight, the allocation writes itself. If it is five families of varying size, work out who has young children — they want the villa with the shortest walk and the earliest bedtime — and build outward from there.

Within a villa, the lock-off studio does more work than people expect. It has its own entrance and kitchenette, so grandparents, a couple, or teenagers who want their own door can have genuine separation inside a shared unit. Our guide to a villa against two hotel rooms covers why that matters more than the nightly rate.

How meals work across three kitchens

Three full kitchens is the right amount of kitchen for 24 people, and the wrong way to use them is to cook three separate dinners every night.

The pattern that works: breakfast happens independently in each villa, which is when a group most wants to be apart anyway, and dinner rotates. Nominate one villa as the host for a given evening, cook there for everyone who wants in, and let the other two stay quiet. Nobody cooks for 24 more than twice in a week, and the kitchenettes cover coffee, snacks and a child who will only eat toast. Our kitchen guide goes into what a real kitchen gives a group, and the cost guide for eight scales to three villas straightforwardly.

One practical note: do a single large grocery run on the way in from the airport and split it across the three units. A resort marketplace is fine for milk and not for a week of food for twenty-four. The grocery guide covers where to stop.

Where a group of 24 eats out together

This is the logistics problem groups discover too late. Most village restaurants are built around tables of four to eight, and a party of 24 is not a reservation — it is an event.

In Canyons Village, The Farm takes standard online reservations for parties of up to 12 and routes anything larger through a separate private-events process. It releases reservations in batches rather than opening a whole season at once, with each tranche going live at 6 pm local time, so a group of this size should find the release date covering its own week and diarise it rather than discover in March that the table has gone. Edge Steakhouse sits inside Westgate itself, so a group dinner without going outside is possible. Beyond the village, Visit Park City maintains a group dining list of venues that can take 25 or more, with several willing to reserve an entire room.

The realistic plan for a week is one or two organised group dinners booked well ahead, with the rest of the evenings either cooked in or split across smaller tables. Our Canyons Village dining guide covers the everyday options.

One point of contact, and why it matters

Booking three units through an agency usually means three reservations, three cancellation policies and nobody who can see the whole picture. Because these villas are rented directly by the owners, one conversation covers all three: the same dates, the same terms, and a single person to ask when the group changes size in January, which it will.

That matters most for the question groups always ask late — whether the units are near each other, and who is where. It is answerable in one message rather than three.

What a group this size should sort before arrival

Four things, in rough order of how badly they bite if missed. Book ski rentals and lift tickets for everyone at once, because twenty-four sets of equipment on the first morning is a two-hour queue otherwise. Expect staggered arrivals and agree who holds keys for the late flight. Plan the grocery run as a task with a named owner. And check the resort fee: it is charged per unit, so a group of 24 pays it three times, which is a line worth knowing before it appears at checkout rather than after.

Large group ski rentals in Park City: common questions

How many people can stay across the Park City villas?

Up to 24. Each Premier Two-Bedroom Villa is 1,100 square feet and sleeps eight, on two king beds and two queen sleeper sofas, and we have three of them in the same resort. Eight, sixteen or twenty-four are the workable group sizes.

How does a group of 24 split across three villas?

Split by household rather than by headcount. Each villa holds eight across two bedrooms and two living areas, with its own front door and bathroom pair, so a family or couple group of six to eight is the natural unit. Avoid splitting one family across two villas.

Does each villa have its own kitchen?

Yes. Each villa has a full kitchen on the main side and a kitchenette in the lock-off studio. Across three villas that is three full kitchens, which is enough to cook for 24 if you nominate one villa as the kitchen for group meals and use the others for breakfast.

Where can a group of 20 or more eat together in Canyons Village?

Not easily without arranging it. The Farm in Canyons Village takes standard online reservations for parties of up to 12 and handles anything larger as a private event. Visit Park City keeps a group dining list of venues that can seat 25 or more, several of which will reserve the whole room.

Can a large group book all three villas for the same week?

Yes, when all three are open for that week. Because the villas are rented directly by the owners rather than through an agency, one enquiry covers all three and you deal with one person rather than three separate bookings.

What should a large group arrange before arriving in Park City?

One grocery run on the way from the airport, ski rentals and lift tickets booked ahead for everyone, any group dinner reserved weeks out, and a plan for staggered arrivals. Check the resort fee as well, because it is charged per unit and a group pays it three times.
