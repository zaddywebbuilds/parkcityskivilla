The Premier Two-Bedroom Villa at Westgate Park City Resort & Spa gives you 1,100 square feet of living space. Two complete bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and two separate living areas mean eight guests have room to spread out after a day on the mountain.

Each bedroom holds a king bed dressed in luxury linens. Each living area holds a leather queen sleeper sofa. The kitchen is fully equipped: center island, premium stone countertops, GE Monogram Series stainless steel appliances, a full range with oven, a two-zone wine and beverage chiller, plus a secondary kitchenette.

The two private bathrooms each include a walk-in steam shower. One adds a separate soaking tub. An in-unit washer and dryer means you can pack for a week and actually live in it, inside a resort with ski-in/ski-out access to the largest ski resort in the United States.

What the rest of Westgate adds

The villa is yours for the week. The resort around it is what most Park City rentals cannot offer: an award-winning spa, two restaurants, a market, pools open year round, and somewhere for the kids to go.

Spa

Best of State winner for Best Destination Spa every year since 2017. Fourteen treatment rooms, a waterfall, salon, massage and body treatments, on the fourth floor of the Elk Building.

Dining

Pools

Families

A family activities programme that changes monthly, and an on-site arcade a few steps from the villa for the evenings nobody wants to go back out.

Availability

Presidents' Week is the hardest week to book in Park City

Every February, families across the country plan months in advance for Presidents' Day week. Schools close. Flights fill. Park City Mountain Resort hits peak lift traffic. Nightly rates for comparable villas on VRBO and Airbnb reach $800-$1,200+ during this window, because demand is high and supply is limited.
