The Villa

1,100 square feet,
two bedrooms, sleeps eight

The Premier Two-Bedroom Villa at Westgate Park City Resort & Spa gives you 1,100 square feet of living space. Two complete bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and two separate living areas mean eight guests have room to spread out after a day on the mountain.

Each bedroom holds a king bed dressed in luxury linens. Each living area holds a leather queen sleeper sofa. The kitchen is fully equipped: stone countertops, stainless appliances, a full range with oven, plus a second kitchenette in the studio half.

The two private bathrooms each include a walk-in steam shower. One adds a separate soaking tub. An in-unit washer and dryer means you can pack for a week and actually live in it, inside a resort with ski-in/ski-out access to one of the largest ski resorts in the United States.

The Resort

The villa is yours for the week. The resort around it is what most Park City rentals cannot offer: an award-winning spa, two restaurants, a market and liquor store, pools open year round, on-site childcare and ski rentals at the door.

Spa

Best of State winner for Best Destination Spa every year since 2017. Fourteen treatment rooms, a waterfall, salon, massage and body treatments, on the fourth floor of the Elk Building.

Pools

Availability

Two prime ski weeks, open for 2027

Every February, families across the country plan months in advance for Presidents' Day week. Schools close. Flights fill. Park City Mountain Resort hits peak lift traffic. Ski-in villas that sleep eight are limited, and the ones at Canyons Village go first.

We own units 1 and 2 at Westgate Park City Resort. Together they form this Premier Two-Bedroom Villa. Available February 13-20, 2027 for Presidents’ Week and March 21-28, 2027 for spring break, booked as a single two-bedroom unit only.
