The Premier Two-Bedroom Villa at Westgate Park City Resort & Spa gives you 1,100 square feet of living space. Two complete bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and two separate living areas mean eight guests have room to spread out after a day on the mountain.

Each bedroom holds a king bed dressed in luxury linens. Each living area holds a leather queen sleeper sofa. The kitchen is fully equipped: center island, premium stone countertops, GE Monogram Series stainless steel appliances, a full range with oven, a two-zone wine and beverage chiller, plus a secondary kitchenette. You can cook real meals here.

The two private bathrooms each include a walk-in steam shower. One adds a separate soaking tub. An in-unit washer and dryer means you can pack for a week and actually live in it. All of this sits inside Westgate Park City Resort & Spa, with ski-in/ski-out access to Park City Mountain Resort, the largest ski resort in the United States.

Presidents' Week is the hardest week to book in Park City

Every February, families across the country plan months in advance for Presidents' Day week. Schools close. Flights fill. Park City Mountain Resort hits peak lift traffic. Nightly rates for comparable villas on VRBO and Airbnb reach $800-$1,200+ during this window, because demand is high and supply is limited.
